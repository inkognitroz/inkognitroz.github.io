# Monetization (MMIR)

MMIR is designed to support many revenue paths over time:

- Subscription micro-SaaS tools
- Paid dashboards and internal workflow apps
- Premium template packs and starter kits
- Excel/CSV automation products
- Client portal implementations and retainers
- Paid AI workflow and automation services
- Affiliate-backed resources and tooling pages

For v1, keep the public site static and focus on clear offers and roadmap visibility.
