# CLAUDE

Repository purpose: **MMIR.ai** - the public product surface for the trusted AI orchestration/control-plane platform.

Working rules:

- Keep changes practical, user-centered and GitHub Pages-compatible.
- Treat `public/mmir.html` and the MMIR chat portal as the primary product journey.
- Preserve the first user journey: open, connect local AI, install, ready, chat.
- Respect `docs/MMIR_CONTROL_PLANE_BOUNDARY.md` and `docs/MMIR_SECURITY_BASELINE.md`.
- Keep secrets, provider keys, billing authority and private organization data out of the public frontend.
- Do not reintroduce retired app-factory branding or strategy.
- Prefer small, testable changes with smoke-check evidence.
